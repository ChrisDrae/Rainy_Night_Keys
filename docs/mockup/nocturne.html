How the color works

Each knob still owns a color in the light behind the controls. The hues moved toward night: warm reds, magenta, violet and deep blue on navy, with grain over everything.

What changed from Lightbox

  • Everything is on one screen, in soft dark tiles. There are no drawers. Only Warmth, Bark, Body and Space have a ▾ for fine controls.
  • The big three use thin outline knobs with a red pointer. The others are red ring knobs. Lit tick marks show the value.
  • Tine and Bell is a vertical Blend slider, and the bell's options have their own panel. Level is the slider on the right.
  • Patina gets three toggles. Motion and Space get dropdowns. Tune and Touch moved into ☰.

Try it

  • Drag knobs and sliders up or down. Hold Shift for fine steps, double-click to reset.
  • Play with the mouse (lower on a key is louder) or A to ' on your keyboard. Z and X shift the octave, and holding Space is the pedal.
  • The sound is a small browser synth standing in for the real engine.
