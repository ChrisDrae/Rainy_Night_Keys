How the color works

Every knob owns a hue. Turning it changes the sound and tints the picture by the same amount, so the window shows what you are hearing.

KnobSound, 0 to 100Picture

Try it

  • Drag a knob up or down. Hold Shift for fine steps, double-click to reset, or use the scroll wheel and arrow keys.
  • Click a knob's name to open its drawer of fine controls. The Tine and Bell labels open the bell drawer.
  • Play with the mouse (lower on a key is louder) or with A to ' on your keyboard. W E T Y U are the black keys, Z and X shift the octave, and holding Space is the sustain pedal.
  • ◂ and ▸ step through the factory presets, so you can watch the picture change with them.

About this draft

The sound is a small browser synth standing in for the real engine, so the knobs have something to color. The plugin itself plays the modeled demo tines plus real celeste and vibraphone samples, and later your own Mk II recordings.

Window: 1000 × 640 at 100%, scalable from 75% to 150%. The keyboard strip folds away in the plugin and is open by default in the standalone app.

Fonts: Fraunces, Jost and DM Mono, all under the SIL Open Font License, so the plugin can bundle them.
